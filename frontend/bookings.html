Bookings & Schedules

Optional Schedule Management

Admins and Laboratory Staff can create laboratory schedules when needed. Creating a schedule every day is not required. Booking requests made on dates without schedules will be sent to the Admin for review.

Request a Laboratory Booking

Select a laboratory and any future date. You may choose your own start and end times. Multiple bookings are allowed on the same day as long as their time periods do not overlap.

Automatic booking decisions:
  • Time fits an available laboratory schedule: Automatically Approved.
  • Schedules exist, but your requested time does not fit: Rejected.
  • No schedule exists for that date: Pending Admin review.
  • Overlapping bookings: Rejected.
Select a laboratory and date to view its schedules.
